kickFile

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026 · applies to kickFile 1.0 for Android, macOS, Windows and Linux

In short: kickFile collects no data. Files go directly from one of your devices to another over your local network, encrypted. There is no kickFile server, no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

1. Information we collect

None. kickFile does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information. The developer never receives your files, file names, device names or any usage data.

2. What stays on your devices

  • Your files are sent directly between your devices on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot, over an encrypted (TLS) connection. They never pass through the internet or any server.
  • Device name and settings (this device's name, where files are saved, theme) are stored on the device.
  • Trusted devices: the name, platform and certificate fingerprint of devices you chose to trust, so they can send to you again without asking.
  • Recent transfers: file names, sizes and times of recent transfers, so you can find received files. You can clear them in the app.

Uninstalling kickFile removes all of this. Files you received stay where they were saved.

3. What other devices on your network can see

While kickFile is open and set to Visible, it announces this device's name, platform and port on the local network so your other devices can find it. Anyone on the same network can see that announcement. Turn off Visible in the app to stop it; trusted devices can still reach you. No one can send you files without your approval unless you trusted their device.

4. Permissions

  • Network and Wi-Fi state, multicast: to find your devices and transfer files on the local network.
  • Camera (Android, optional): only to scan a kickFile QR code. Images are processed on the device and never stored.
  • Notifications and foreground service (Android): to show transfer progress and keep a transfer going while the screen is off.
  • Photos and media location (Android): to save received photos and videos to your gallery with their original details.
  • Local network (macOS): to find and reach your devices.

5. Diagnostic logs

kickFile keeps a short technical log on the device to help fix problems. It contains no file names or file contents. It leaves your device only if you choose Export logs and share the file yourself.

6. Children

kickFile has no accounts and collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/ukpratik/kickfile/issues.
